HELL OHIO

No expectations. No judgement. Only sound.

This event promises to be the most memorable renegade rave of 2026. Lose yourself to the sound of underground breakbeats, electro, and techno.

Rave Location: 39°57’57.5”N 83°01’01.4”W

Parking Address: The Boat House, 679 W Spring St Columbus, OH 43215

Park in the lot for the Boat House. Follow the scioto trail east over the thin bridge. You will see bits of caution tape tied to the bridge. Follow these down to a path in the woods. From there, follow the path of glowsticks until you can hear the music.
